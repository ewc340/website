A browser-based control panel for queuing VQE experiments, visualizing convergence curves, and comparing ansätze across runs. Built as a thin Next.js front end over a FastAPI orchestration layer.

The goal was operator ergonomics: researchers should see job status, energy traces, and parameter snapshots without SSH-ing into the cluster for every poll.
